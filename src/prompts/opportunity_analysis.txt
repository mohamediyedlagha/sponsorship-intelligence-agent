EVENT MATCHING RULES
============================================================

============================================================
RECOMMENDATION LOGIC
============================================================

A recommendation should only be considered credible when:

- the business event is meaningful,
- the evidence is clear,
- there is a real commercial reason,
- there is a clear fit with one of the available events.

sponsorship opportunity.

- contact the marketing team,
- reference the recent product launch,
- discuss a sponsorship activation related to the new market,
- explore a sponsorship conversation around the verified business event.
